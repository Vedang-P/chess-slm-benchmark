\begin{abstract}
  % Fill from docs/capability-analysis.md after the Kaggle run.
  Small language models (SLMs, 1.5--4B) are increasingly deployed as on-device agents, yet
  nearly every benchmark of game-playing ability -- LLM Chess, ChessArena, ChessQA,
  ChessBench, grid-game leaderboards -- evaluates frontier-scale models. We present the first
  systematic measurement of SLM chess ability across a multi-task battery: move legality on
  standard positions, mate-in-1 and mate-in-2 tactics (real lichess puzzles), best-move
  accuracy against Stockfish, exact endgame play on retrograde-solved 3x3/5x5 boards, and
  full-game playout vs a random opponent -- in two input representations (rendered grid vs
  FEN notation) -- plus tic-tac-toe and Connect-4 for cross-game generalization. Every metric
  uses external oracles; no model self-judgment.
\end{abstract}
