# Workshop Information

## Efficient and On-Device AI Agents Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026

### Key Details
- **Venue**: NeurIPS 2026 Workshop, Sydney, Australia
- **Date**: December 11-12, 2026
- **Deadline**: August 29, 2026 (AoE)
- **Submission Portal**: OpenReview (opens July 15, 2026)
- **Notification**: September 26, 2026
- **Camera-ready**: October 10, 2026

### Overview
"Can AI agents run on your phone? Efficient architectures, on-device reasoning, and multi-agent systems for real-world edge deployment."

### Topics of Interest
- Efficient model architectures for on-device agents (quantization, pruning, SLMs)
- Training and fine-tuning under constraints (RL, instruction tuning, PEFT)
- Efficient reasoning and planning (CoT compression, speculative decoding, lightweight planning)
- Memory management and context compression (KV-cache, RAG at edge)
- Multi-agent orchestration on-device
- Tool use and function calling under latency constraints
- Benchmarks and evaluation for on-device agents
- Privacy-preserving and secure on-device agents
- Real-world applications (mobile, robotics, wearables, IoT)

### Submission Format
- **Short Paper**: 4 pages + unlimited references
- **Long Paper**: 9 pages + unlimited references
- Double-blind review, NeurIPS 2026 LaTeX template
- **Non-archival** (can resubmit to other venues)
- Best Paper Award + Spotlight Presentations
